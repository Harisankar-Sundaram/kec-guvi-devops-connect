

# KEC × GUVI-HCL DevOps Event Website

A professional, clean event/promotional website for the DevOps Practical Teaching collaboration between **KEC College** and **GUVI (HCL Company)** for CSE students.

---

## Page 1: Home / Landing Page
- **Hero Section** with a bold banner featuring KEC and GUVI logos side-by-side, event title ("DevOps Practical Teaching Program"), tagline, and a CTA button linking to the Schedule page
- **About the Event** section explaining the collaboration — KEC CSE department partnering with GUVI-HCL to provide hands-on DevOps training
- **Highlights** section with icon cards: Hands-on Labs, Industry Experts, Certification, Real-world Projects
- **College & Company Section** with images/photos of KEC College campus and GUVI/HCL branding, along with brief descriptions of each organization
- **Footer** with contact info, social links, and copyright

## Page 2: Schedule & Syllabus
- **Event Details** banner: dates, venue, duration, eligibility (KEC CSE students)
- **Class Schedule** displayed as a clean timeline or table — topics like Linux Basics, Git & GitHub, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD Pipelines, Cloud Deployment
- **Instructors/Trainers** section with placeholder cards for GUVI trainers
- **Venue Info** with a KEC College image and address details

## Design & Branding
- Professional & clean layout with a color palette inspired by KEC (blue/white) and GUVI (green/orange accents)
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop
- Placeholder images for KEC campus and GUVI branding (user can replace with real photos later)
- Smooth navigation bar with links to both pages
